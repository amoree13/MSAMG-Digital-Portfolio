## About This Activity

This activity focused on **color palettes and typography** and how they affect the appearance and readability of a design. I explored how different colors work together and how font choices can help establish the mood and visual hierarchy of an output. I wanted the different elements to look coordinated rather than simply choosing colors and fonts because they looked nice on their own.

## What Have I Learned?

I learned that choosing a color palette and typography takes more thought than I initially expected. One of the difficulties I experienced was deciding which colors and fonts looked good together, especially when working under time pressure. I addressed this by trying different combinations and paying more attention to contrast, readability, and consistency. This activity helped me understand that even simple design choices can affect how people understand the information being presented. In the future, I want to be more confident in selecting a palette and type style before starting a design so I can work more efficiently.
