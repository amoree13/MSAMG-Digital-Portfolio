## 05. Learning Through Challenges

Not everything came easily to me throughout this subject, especially when I had to work with unfamiliar digital tools.

One of the biggest challenges I encountered was **learning how to use GitHub**. At first, I found it confusing to understand repositories, folders, files, and README formatting. I had to explore the platform, follow instructions carefully, and figure things out one step at a time.

Another challenge was **creating designs in Canva under time pressure**. I enjoy making my outputs visually appealing, but choosing the right color palette, arranging elements, and deciding on an overall design can sometimes take me longer than expected. There were moments when I wasn't sure what looked good together, so I had to experiment with different combinations and layouts.

These experiences taught me that learning technology requires patience, practice, and a willingness to make mistakes. I may not understand everything immediately, but I am learning to become more comfortable with trying things on my own.
