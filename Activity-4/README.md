## About This Activity

For our **Midterm Presentation Exam** in GE 4120: 21st Century IT Skills, our group was assigned to discuss **Google Docs**, a cloud-based word-processing application that allows users to create, edit, organize, and collaborate on documents online.

Our presentation covered the different features and functions of Google Docs, including document creation, formatting, sharing permissions, real-time collaboration, comments, suggesting mode, and version history. We also discussed how these features can be useful in academic work and other situations that require digital collaboration.

To make our reporting more engaging, we also prepared an **interactive activity** that allowed our classmates to participate and test their understanding of Google Docs.

## Presentation Design & Creative Process

For our presentation, we used a **retro computer-inspired design** with blue, black, white, and gray as our main colors. We incorporated computer-related graphics, pixel-style cursors, window frames, and other digital elements to complement our topic.

We wanted the presentation to be creative while still keeping the discussion organized and understandable. We divided the content into different sections and maintained a consistent visual style throughout the slides.

We also applied presentation design principles such as **visual hierarchy, contrast, alignment, and consistency** to help guide the audience through the discussion.

## What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned that Google Docs offers more than just basic typing and document editing. Preparing for our presentation helped me understand its collaboration features, sharing permissions, and version history, which are especially useful when working on group requirements.

One challenge was organizing the amount of information we needed to present while making sure our slides were not too overwhelming. We addressed this by dividing the discussion into sections and keeping the design consistent. I also realized how important teamwork, communication, and preparation are when working on a group presentation.

In the future, I can apply what I learned by using Google Docs more effectively for academic requirements, collaborative projects, and document preparation. As a nursing student, I believe these skills will also be useful when preparing reports, sharing information, and working with others in a professional setting.
