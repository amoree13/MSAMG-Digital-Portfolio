## About This Activity

For this activity, I worked on an infographic about **financial blind spots in student spending**. I chose this topic because small everyday purchases—such as food, transportation, and school expenses—can add up without us noticing. I wanted to explain the problem in a way that other students could easily relate to, using simple text, icons, and organized visual sections.

The project also explored possible technology-based solutions. These included a **basic expense-tracking spreadsheet** that could calculate totals and monitor budget limits, as well as the idea of a **financial tracking app** that could organize transactions and provide reminders or spending summaries. These were proposed concepts, not a completed working application.

## My Design Choices

I used **blue as the main color** to give the infographic a more organized and informative appearance. I also used sections, icons, and diagrams to make the content easier to follow without relying on long paragraphs. I created the infographic using **Canva**, with attention to visual hierarchy and spacing.

## What Have I Learned?

This activity taught me how important it is to simplify information before turning it into an infographic. One challenge was deciding which details to include because I wanted the output to be informative without looking overcrowded. I worked through this by focusing on the key points and arranging them into sections supported by visuals. I also learned how a digital project can connect an everyday student problem to a possible technology-based solution. In the future, I can apply these skills when creating health education materials or presenting other information that needs to be clear and easy to understand.
