## About This Activity

For this activity, I explored how improving a prompt can affect the quality of responses generated by **ChatGPT**. I chose **NCLEX-RN Study Strategies** as my topic because I am currently a fourth-year nursing student, and I wanted to learn how I could begin preparing for the examination while still managing my classes and clinical duties.

I started by asking ChatGPT for effective NCLEX study strategies and a manageable study routine. Although the first response provided helpful suggestions, I wanted something more detailed and practical.

I then asked ChatGPT to improve my original prompt by making the instructions clearer and more specific. After using the improved prompt, I compared both responses based on their **clarity, detail, accuracy, and usefulness**.

## My Prompt Improvement Process

### Original Prompt

> Hi! I'm currently a fourth-year nursing student, and I want to start preparing for the NCLEX-RN. I'm not really sure where to begin or what subjects I should prioritize. Can you suggest some effective study strategies and a manageable study routine, considering that I still have classes and clinical duties? Please explain it in a simple and understandable way.

### Improved Prompt

For the improved prompt, I provided more specific instructions about what I wanted ChatGPT to include, such as:

- A realistic four-week introductory NCLEX study schedule
- Effective study techniques, including active recall and spaced repetition
- Strategies for answering prioritization, delegation, and NGN questions
- A manageable daily study routine
- Ways to identify weak areas and monitor improvement
- Practical advice for avoiding burnout

By providing more context and a clearer expected format, the second prompt generated a more structured and practical response.

## Comparing the AI Responses

| Criteria | Original Response | Improved Response |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Clarity** | Easy to understand but mostly general recommendations | Better organized with clearly separated sections |
| **Detail** | Provided basic study strategies without a specific plan | Included schedules, techniques, and practical examples |
| **Accuracy** | Generally appropriate advice but limited NCLEX-specific explanations | Included more specific NCLEX concepts, although important details still require verification |
| **Usefulness** | Helpful for understanding where to begin | More practical for developing an actual study routine |

Overall, I found the **improved response more helpful** because it gave me a clearer idea of how to organize my NCLEX preparation while balancing my responsibilities as a nursing student.

## What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned that the way I write my prompts can greatly affect the quality of the responses I receive from AI. My original prompt already included some background information, but I noticed that the response was still quite general and did not provide a specific study plan.

One challenge was figuring out what additional instructions I needed to include to get a more useful answer. I addressed this by asking ChatGPT to improve my prompt and specify the information, examples, and format I wanted.

The improved response helped me understand how a clearer and more detailed prompt can make AI-generated information more organized and practical. I also realized that I should not rely entirely on AI, especially when studying nursing topics, because the information still needs to be checked for accuracy.

In the future, I can apply what I learned when creating nursing reviewers, understanding difficult topics, preparing for examinations, and using AI more effectively as a study tool.
