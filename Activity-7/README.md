# Activity 07 — Comparative Résumé Generation Using AI

### ChatGPT vs. Gemini

*Exploring how artificial intelligence can help create professional documents.* 

✦ ────────── ✦ ────────── ✦

</div>

## About This Activity

For this activity, I used **ChatGPT and Google Gemini** to generate a professional one-page résumé for an entry-level **Operating Room (OR) Nurse position**.

I provided both AI tools with the same prompt, which included my personal information, educational background, clinical rotations, nursing competencies, and seminars attended.

After generating the two résumés, I compared their **structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality** to determine which output was more suitable for my target position.

## AI Tools Used

| AI Tool | Purpose |
| :-- | :-- |
| **ChatGPT** | Generated a résumé using my personal and academic information. |
| **Google Gemini** | Generated another résumé using the same instructions for comparison. |

## Comparing the Outputs

Both AI tools produced professional-looking résumés, but I noticed differences in how they organized and presented the information.

**ChatGPT** included more of my educational background and references, making the output detailed but somewhat crowded.

**Gemini**, on the other hand, placed more emphasis on my Operating Room experience, relevant nursing skills, and career objective. I preferred its layout because the information was more focused on the position I wanted to apply for.

I decided to use Gemini's output as the starting point for my final résumé while making adjustments to improve its clarity and readability.

## What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned that different AI tools can generate different results even when given exactly the same instructions. One challenge I encountered was deciding which résumé was more appropriate because both outputs had their own strengths and weaknesses. I addressed this by comparing their structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality rather than simply choosing the one that looked better.

I realized that a good résumé is not necessarily the one with the most information, but the one that presents the most relevant qualifications clearly and professionally. This activity also taught me the importance of writing specific prompts and reviewing AI-generated content carefully.

In the future, I can apply what I learned when preparing professional documents, improving my résumé, and using AI tools more responsibly in my academic and nursing career.

## My Final Résumé

After reviewing both outputs, I prepared an improved one-page résumé tailored to an entry-level Operating Room Nurse position.
