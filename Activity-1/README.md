## About This Activity

For our first activity, I created a **Free Coffee** poster while practicing the basic principles of presentation design. I chose a photo of **Arrupe Hall** as the background to make the poster feel more connected to the actual location. My goal was to make the information easy to notice and understand while still keeping the layout visually appealing.

## Design Principles I Applied

- **Visual hierarchy** — I made the most important information noticeable first.
- **Contrast** — I considered how the text stood out against the background photo.
- **Alignment** — I arranged the details to keep the poster organized.
- **Proximity** — I grouped related information so it would be easier to follow.

## What Have I Learned?

I learned that creating a poster is not just about making it look nice; it is also about making the message clear. This activity was challenging for me because I do not consider myself naturally artistic, and we had limited time to finish the design. I tried to work through this by focusing on the basic design principles instead of adding too many elements. Looking back, there are still some details I would improve, especially the arrangement and overall balance of the poster. In the future, I can apply what I learned to make clearer presentations, educational materials, and other visual outputs.
